# Visual Reference Register

Research date: **2026-11-13, America/New_York**. Sources inform composition and architectural checks; no source photograph was pasted into the generated images or supplied as an image-generation input. Linked photographs retain their owners' rights; this file is a source register, not a redistribution license.

## Philadelphia

- [Schuylkill Banks — Boardwalk](https://www.schuylkillbanks.org/landmarks/boardwalk): project operator; Locust Street to South Street Bridge, eastern bank. Check a low over-water deck parallel to the bank and bridge access, rather than a generic stone embankment.
- [Operator's anniversary account and photographs](https://www.schuylkillbanks.org/blog/happy-9th-anniversary-boardwalk): concrete surface resembling planks, solar lamps, overlooks and a deck just above the waterline. [Photograph](https://www.schuylkillbanks.org/sites/default/files/styles/full_xs/public/2024-01/IMG_0194.jpg?h=d05b6ae0&itok=W-tasoTe).
- [Operator's trail map](https://www.schuylkillbanks.org/explore/map): establish the true sequence of river crossings and the boardwalk before final drawing. No claim that the sketch's low bridge precisely reproduces South Street Bridge.

## Nanjing

- [Zhongshan Mountain administration — plane-tree avenues](https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202506/t20250603_5575798.html): tree character and the mountain setting; explicitly includes Linggusi Road, not a survey of Zhongshan East Road.
- [Nanjing government — Purple Mountain / avenue imagery](https://english.nanjing.gov.cn/WhatsNew/Latest/202509/t20250922_5653480.html): Lingyuan Road and wooded mountain context. Lingyuan Road must not be attributed to Zhongshan East Road.
- [Zhongshan Mountain attractions](https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/ForeignLanguages/202609/t20260904_5905433.html): mountain on the eastern outskirts; keep the distant landform wooded and low rather than alpine.
- [Nanjing City Wall official site](https://english.njcitywall.cn/?obj=ticket): named wall sections including Zhongshan Gate; final work still requires selecting one actual segment and checking its gate form.
- [Public photograph of Zhongshan East Road plane trees](https://www.sohu.com/a/695376609_100106747): supplementary streetscape observation, not measured geometry. [Image](https://p5.itc.cn/images01/20230707/41a5ff2e196140faa3694cd57e72d2a1.jpeg). Verify image location independently before precise final tracing.
- [Scenic-area tree-canopy photographs](https://zschina.nanjing.gov.cn/lyzx/202511/t20251106_5684280.html): branch rhythm and canopy only; do not conflate this scenic-area walk with the urban Zhongshan Road.

## New York City

- [NYC DOT — Brooklyn Bridge](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/html/infrastructure/brooklyn-bridge.shtml): primary structural reference; main span 1,595.5 ft (about 486.3 m), central clearance 135 ft, lengthy landward approaches. [Official photograph](https://www.nyc.gov/html/dot/images/infrastructure/brooklyn-bridge.jpg).
- [Brooklyn Bridge Park — Brooklyn Bridge](https://brooklynbridgepark.org/places-to-see/brooklyn-bridge/): pointed Gothic arches and park-side viewing relationship.
- [US National Archives — bridge registration discussion](https://text-message.blogs.archives.gov/2022/01/18/nrhp-bridges/): masonry towers approximately 276 ft 6 in (84.3 m) above high water, useful for subsequent perspective blocking.
- [NYC landmark guide](https://a856-centre360.nyc.gov/LandmarkHunt): distinguishes the buff masonry Brooklyn Bridge from the steel Manhattan Bridge commonly framed in DUMBO photographs.
- [Empire Fulton Ferry photograph and authorship record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_of_Pebble_Beach_from_Empire_Fulton_Ferry_Park.jpg): waterfront spatial context only. Final building references should lock a specific Water Street / Empire Stores frontage; the sketches' repetitive brick blocks are type studies, not exact facade replicas.

## Guangzhou

- [Guangzhou official city guide — historic architecture](https://www.eguangzhou.gov.cn/gzspecialreports/139thCantonFair/content/post_42761.html): separates Xiguan residences, Yongqing Fang / Enning Road qilou, Nanhua West and Chen Clan Ancestral Hall. Covered ground-floor walkways are essential to qilou; not merely decorative arches on a facade. [Qilou photograph linked from guide](https://mediapic.oss-cn-shenzhen.aliyuncs.com/mediapic/2026041422/fb36434fbb084c178cbcf2b7db8b33da_nfrmbig.jpg).
- [Guangzhou Culture and Tourism Bureau — Chen Clan Ancestral Hall](https://wglj.gz.gov.cn/ztmb/gzhyn/ajjq/4a/content/post_9773088.html): address at Enlongli, Zhongshan Seventh Road; courtyard-linked hall complex with ceramic, brick, wood and stone decoration. [Official image](https://wglj.gz.gov.cn/img/1/1242/1242474/9773088.jpg).
- [Built Heritage Summit proceedings](https://www.amo.gov.hk/filemanager/amo/common/form/2022_AMO_Summit-EN.pdf): supplementary heritage context for the Chen Clan complex. It is not an original courtyard within Enning Road's qilou block.
