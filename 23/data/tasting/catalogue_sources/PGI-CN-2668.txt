5. Description of product

with sp ecific processing technology within the scope of protection of geographical 
indications, with unique quality characteristics. Puer Cha (Pu’er tea) divided into Puer 
Cha (Pu'er tea) (raw tea), Puer Cha (Pu'er tea) (cooked tea) two types according to its 
processing technology and quality characteristics. The finished tea can be classified as:

Specific Production Steps: 
(1) Sunning tea: 
Tea leaves→ fixation→ rolling→ sunning→ packing 
(2) Pu’er Cha (Pu’er Tea) (raw): 
Refining sunning tea→ steaming and compressing→ drying→ packaging (raw tea) 
(3) Pu’er (cooked) Loose Tea: 
Fermenting sunning tea→ drying→ refining→ packaging (cooked) Loose Tea 
(4) Pu’er (cooked) Compressed Tea: 
Pu’er (cooked) Loose Tea → steaming and compressing → drying→ packaging 
(cooked) Compressed Tea 
7. Link with the Geographical Area 
The raw material suitable for making Pu’er Cha (Pu’er  Tea) is Yunnan big leaf 
tea species. Its  production has a long history, according to one of the earliest written 
records - "Qiu-hu dallying volunteers" Written by Chang Qu in the Eastern Jin 
Dynasty (317-420), Lawrence w. Zhou felling period as early as th ree thousand years 
ago, Yunnan tea ancients PuRen has offered tea to ji fa. According to the Southern 
Song Dynasty Li Shi "  Annals of history" records: " West fan with Pu tea, since the 
Tang Dynasty." West fan, refers to the living in the Tibetan area of t he brothers and 
nationalities, Pu tea is Pu'er tea, it’s clear that there is the trade of Pu'er tea as early as

the Tang Dynasty. Qing Dynasty Zhao Xuemin "Compendium of Materia Medica 
Supplements" wrote Pu’er Cha (Pu’er tea) came from Pu’er Fu Gedeng, Youle, 
Yibang and other six tea mountains. Pu’er Fu was also now Pu’er City, as the town of 
southern Yunnan, around the production of tea transported to Pu'er Fu concentrated 
processing, and then sell all over the Tibetan, This was the source of Pu'er tea name. 
Historically, Yunnan's tea was sold to Tibet, Sichuan, Hong Kong and Southeast 
Asia and other places through a number of ancient tea horse roads. As the traffic was 
inconvenient, the transport of tea is mainly rely on caravan pack transport, for the 
convenience of transport, tend to loose tea autoclave molding, outsourcing Bamboo 
leaves, into the bamboo basket shipment. After a long journey, the chemical 
substances of original suppression of tight tea produced a series of oxidation and 
condensation reaction in the role of hot and humid and microbial, inadvertently 
formed sweet, slip, alcohol, thick quality characteristics.  
Pu’er Cha (Pu’er Tea) for lateritic soil, brick red, red soil, mountain red soil and 
mountain yellow soil, etc; soil organic matter content ≥ 1%; pH value: 4.5 to 6.0. 
Besides, belongs to acidic soils, high organic matter content; Drainage, good air 
permeability, soil deep, very suitable for tea tree growth. Pu- erh tea producing region 
climate type is subtropical monsoon climate, annual averag e temperature 17 to 22 ℃ . 
Annual temperature difference is small, daily temperature difference is big, annual 
rainfall is 1200- 1800 mm, the rainy season, warm and humid; Site conditions at an 
altitude of 1000 m to 2100 m, mountain slope ≤ 25 °, the surface solar radiation is 
more, good light conditions, conducive to the formation of tea polyphenols in tea. 
Pu’er Cha (Pu’er Tea)  producing high forest coverage rate, negative oxygen ion 
content is high in the air, the air quality in good condition. These excellent climatic

conditions were favorable to the growth of Yunnan large leaf tea in these areas, and 
laid a solid foundation for the formation of the chemical quality of Pu'er tea. It was 
well known that the changes of climatic conditions, such as sunshine, temperature and 
precipitation, had obvious effects on the chemical constituents of tea, especially 
secondary metabolites. 
There was a special process of " post-fermentation" during Pu'er tea (cooked) 
producing; it was the most important step to format the quality characteristics of Pu'er 
tea (cooked). The m icroorganisms played an important role in this process, under the 
action of microorganism, enzyme and damp heat, a series of chemical changes 
occurred in Pu'er tea  (raw), oxidation and condensation of tea polyphenols, 
decomposition and degradation of protein and amino acids, consumption and 
decomposition of carbohydrates, and polymerization and condensation of various 
products, which made Pu'er tea (cooked) with red color, sweet flavor, special fragrant 
smell from sunning tea with green color, astringent, fresh and pure. 
Pu'er tea was rated as one of the top ten tea regional public brands in China at the 
first China international tea expo organized by the ministry of agriculture. The brand 
value of Pu'er tea was exceeded 6 billion Yuan for two consecutive years, and ranked 
the first among the top 10 tea brands in the “Evaluation of regional public brand value 
of Chinese tea " in April 2018. There were 45 Pu'er tea production enterprise 
(including instant Pu'e r tea powder ) in our province, and 56 products obtained 
Yunnan famous brand title, accounting for 6 % of the total number of current effective 
Yunnan famous brands.
