TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS 
FOR REGISTRATION OF GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 凤冈锌硒茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Fenggang Xin Xi Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Fenggang Zinc Selenium Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Tea Association in Fenggang County 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Registered as certification mark  on December 7th, 2011 (registration number : 
8585068). 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
The tea leaves, which are of the same size, are tightly tied. The tips can be seen 
clearly. The leaves are grayish -green and smooth. The fragrance is lingering, the 
flavor refreshing and mellow. The drink is bright yellow -green, in which lie evenly 
the light green dregs. The tea is rich in zinc,  selenium and other trace elements. Every 
kilogram of the tea contains 40-100mg zinc and 0.05-2.5mg selenium. 
The specific characteristics of Fenggang Zinc Selenium Tea as defined are due to the 
natural factors in the delimited geographical area. 
6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Yongan, Xinjian, Tuxi, Suiyang, Longquan, Huaping, Shijing, Yonghe, Wangzhai, 
Heba, Jinhua, Ya chua, Yanfeng and Tianqiao in Fenggang County (14 towns and 
townships altogether). The area is located  107°31´and 107°56 ´east longitude, and 
27°32´and 28°21´north latitude.

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
 Fenggang County has a good ecology. 56% of Fenggang County is covered 
with forests. The average elevation is 720 meters, the annual average temperature 
15.2℃, and the annual average rainfall 1200mm. The area is mountainous. The soil 
contains trace element s like zinc and selenium, which is favorable to the special 
qualities of Fenggang Zinc Selenium Tea. The zinc content in soil in Fenggang county 
was 95.3 mg/ kg , the selenium content is 2.5mg/ kg , the zinc and selenium contained 
in tea are completely deriv ed from the natural adsorption of zinc and selenium in the 
soil.
