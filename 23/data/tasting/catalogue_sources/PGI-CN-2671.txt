6. Concise definition of the geographical area 
Tanyang Gong Fu’s geographical indication is limited to administration area of 
fu’an. 
The specific production procedures of Tanyang Gong Fu: ①  Select from the 
aforesaid range a suitable location for planting tea bushes and picki ng raw tea leaves; 
② Preliminary processes: tea leaves → withering →kneading → fermentation → 
drying (shaping); ③  Refining processes: screening → piling → shaping →blending 
→averaging→ re-drying; ④ Storing: The Tea after refined would be the finished 
products with package, they are stored in the special warehouse that is neat 、dry and 
cool with no smell. 
These production procedures are made in the specified geographic area.  
7. Link with the Geographical Area 
As the “Hometown of Chinese Tea”, Fu’an is fit fo r tea production in 
geographical situation, climate, soil, etc. 
Tanyang Gong Fu is one of the three kinds of famous “Min Hong”. It has a long 
history, widely known at home and abroad. 
As the “Hometown of Chinese Tea”, Fu’an is located in the northeast of f ujian 
province. The area includes the southeast slope JiuFeng mountains, Taimu mountains 
southwest、donggong mountains southeast extension; between 26°41′~ 27°24′ north 
latitude and 119°23′~ 119°52′east longitude. Fu’an according to mountain alongside 
sea，The landscape is dominated by Middle and low mountains and hills 。Cross the

Tanyang Gong Fu was created in 1851，own more than 150 years history. In Xianfeng

then “Tanyang Gong Fu” was famous all around the world.
