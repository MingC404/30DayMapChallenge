7. Link with the geographical area 
   Anxi Tie Guan Yin  is a kind of Oolong Tea which has a consummate and 
traditional processing flow, is known as an appearance of “green pedicle, green 
abdomen and dragonfly head, lingering fragrance after seven brewing” and very 
famous because of its “YIN flavour ” integrated embodiment of flavor and aroma as a 
peculiar quality characteristics of Tie Guan Yin tea varieties. It is obviously diffe rent

from the tea products of other tea producing areas; the products are black bloom, 
shiny, fat and bold, tight and straight and heavy as if made of iron; the liquor color is

sweet and fresh, promoting the secretion of saliva or body fluid and having a sweet 
cheek tooth if drinking, known as “green leaves rimmed with red, still fragrant after 
seven bubbles”. The quality characteristics of Anxi Tie Guan Yin are closely rel ated 
with the climate, soil, environment, culture and other factors of Anxi County:

（2）the relationship with climate characteristics 
Chemical compositions of Anxi Tie Guan Yin mainly contain tea polyphenols, 
amino acids, caffeine, tea polysaccharide, pigment, aromatic substances and so on, 
and the formation and accumulation of substances, part of tea plant secondary

metabolites, are inf luenced by tea plant metabolites under the certain condition of 
climate such as light, temperature and precipitation, then have an impact tea quality. 
The climate of Anxi county is subtropical marine monsoon climate; the average 
temperature is 16℃ to 21℃; the annual sunshine time is 1,850 hours to 2,050 hours; 
the time when accumulated temperature of the county overtops 10℃  is 259 days to 
338 days; Frost -free period is 260 days to 350 days; the four seasons are distinct 
throughout the year with no- hot summer and no- cold winter. Anxi county possesses 
the climate conditions which is an important factor to form high quality Tie Guan Yin, 
such as a moderate amount of light, good scattered light, large temperature difference 
between day and night caused by high a ltitude and abundant rainfall caused by the 
wind without interference. The climate conditions are suitable for the natural growth 
of tie guan yin tea trees, conducive to increase of amino acid content and decrease of 
the ratio of tea polyphenols and amino acid, accumulation of tea aroma precursors, 
and increase of aroma substance content including nerolidol, fanny ene, indole, benzyl 
ethanol, anti - 2 - hexene aldehyde in Tie Guan Yin, which characteristics make Anxi 
Tie Guan Yin  obviously phonological and have an appearance of fat and bold, curly 
tight and heavy. The color of clean aroma Tieguanyin tea is green and oily; the liquor 
color is green-yellow and bright; the aroma is clean and high, fresh and brisk and long. 
The color of Heavy flavor Tieguanyin i s black bloom with obviously sand green; the 
liquor color is golden or orange; the aroma is heavy fragrant and long; the taste is 
mellow, sweet and refreshing.

Anxi Tie Guan Yin  has favorable site conditions; the  s oil is mainly 
mountainous and sandy; PH value is 4.5 to 6; it is red soil at an elevation of 700cm or 
less while yellow -red or yellow soil at an elevation of more than 700cm; the soil 
texture is loose; the soil layer is deep; it has a high content of organ ic matter, and is 
rich in mineral nutrients, especially high content of manganese, zinc, molybdenum in 
soil, which is highly desirable for tea growing. Studies have shown that manganese 
can promote the formation of amino acids, vitamins and tea polyphenols  in tea plant, 
zinc can improve the content of carbohydrate which is conducive to the formation of 
amino acids in tea, and molybdenum content was positively correlated with soil pH 
coinciding with growing demand for acidic soil to Tieguanyin plant. Therefore, Anxi 
county soil with high content of manganese, zinc, molybdenum has a non -negligible 
contribution to form the characteristics of high quality tea. 
（4）Relationship with human characteristics 
Anxi is the main tea producing area, with a long history about a thousand years, 
according to “Chinese  classic of tea” and “annals of Anxi County”. Later the local 
growers have found Tie Guan Yin  in the Qing  Yongzheng, dating back  nearly 
280 years of history. Especially in 1978, asexual reproduction technology was 
invented in Anxi, namely “tea tree short cutting seedlings method”. Tieguanyin clonal 
varieties cultivated by the method have good adaptability, stable provenance and 
difficult variation, which excellent characters can be passed from generation to 
generation and provide tieguanyin native provenance for the breeding production. 
Hence, asexual reproduction technology is also an Anxi's great contribution in the 
history of tea in China. The higher agricultural colleges textbook “Manufacture of

Qing yongzheng three to thirteen years(1725- 1735). Tie Guan Yin originates in 
Xiping town, Anxi. There are two kinds of views about it origin, “WANG say” and 
“WEI say”. At the beginning of t he 21st century, Anxi people's  played to the 
collective wisdom to innovate machining process of Tie Guan Yin in traditional 
inheritance, such as controlling the temperature and humidity, reducing the effects of 
bad weather on machining process by the adopt ion of technology and modern 
equipment. These innovations have resolved the tea season problem influenced by 
climatic conditions especially high temperature in the summer, greatly improve the 
quality of summer tea, and have been widely applied in producing  areas of various 
Tie Guan Yin tea. 
Anxi Tie Guan Yin is one of the top ten famous tea in China, favored by 
customers inside and outside the country because of “ YIN flavour” and good quality, 
has been to the world by “Maritime silk road”, sold well both at home and abroad, and 
popular in Europe and the United States, especially southeast Asia as “overseas 
Chinese tea” at the beginning of the 20th century. With the  excellent quality and the 
“YIN flavour”, Anxi Tie Guan Yin is one  of the favorite teas to consumers and has a 
good reputation at home and abroad. According to Xiamen  harbor history: England 
have bought 1,800 to 1,864 tons of Oolong tea such as Anxi Tie Guan Yin ever y year 
from Xiamen harbor between 1858 to 1864, and 4,500 tons in 1877. Since the reform 
and opening up, especially since the 1990 s, Anxi county Party committee, the county 
government pay special attention to the development of Anxi Tie Guan Yin , 
developing “high quality, high- quality goods, famous brand” and “sustainable
