6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
（1）Geographic Area Boundary  
Hengxian Mo Li Hua Cha is produced in the existing administrative region of Heng 
County, Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region.   
（2）Detailed Production Steps   
Process: tea base treatment → fresh flower maintenance →camellia splicing → 
scenting piling → ventilation and scenting adding→ holding out of scented flower → 
drying → re-piling and re-scenting → screening → packing.  
①Tea base treatment: tea base before scenting needs to be dried at a temperature of 
100 ℃ to 110 ℃, with moisture less than 5%, and should be timely cooled after 
drying.   
②Fresh flower maintenance: the fresh Hengxian Mo Li Hua needs to be placed in 
ventilating containers after picking, and maintained in plant in the day. Scenting can 
be started when flower open rate reaches above 80%.  
③Camellia mixing: place green tea to be scented on scenting field, and mix camellia 
in certain proportion: fresh flower /tea leaf (35 - 80kg / 100kg), pile temperature: 38 
to 48 ℃, scenting pile thickness: 35 to 55cm, time ≤ 1.5 hours.  
④Piling a nd scenting: ventilate when pile thickness reaches 35 to 55cm and pile 
temperature 45 to 48 ℃ (first scenting), and hold out flowers after 9 to 14- hour

scenting. Practice according to the quality requirements for finished tea. One -scenting 
and one holding -out can be practiced. The maximum scenting times can reach nine 
and holding-out one time.   
⑤Ventilation and scenting adding: ventilate when pile temperature reaches 38 to 48 ℃ 
in 5 to 6 hours. Re -scenting can be started when the ventilated pile reaches am bient 
temperature with a thickness of 25 to 35cm, and the scenting time can last 5 to 6 hours. 
When tea pile temperature reaches about 36℃ holding-out of scented flower can be 
started.   
⑥Holding out of scented flower: when it is scented for 9 to 14 hours, holding-out 
process should be immediately started, residues need screening out, tea and flower 
need separating.       
⑦Drying: drying temperature should be 100 to 130 ℃, with moisture controlled on 
the needs of re -scenting, holding-out and packing. In order to preserve the aroma and 
the freshness of Hengxian Mo Li Hua Cha, it should be spread out for cooling after 
drying, with tea pile temperature less than or equal to 40℃.  
⑧Re-piling and re-scenting: with the same process as piling and scenting, it needs to 
use big, flush and full matured Hengxian Mo Li Hua with elegant and lofty aroma. 
With a flower content of 8% to 12%, the process lasts 6 to 8 hours.    
⑨Screening: with a mesh number of 5 to 7, it uses sieves to separate tea and flower 
stems, abandon flower and remain tea in order to avoid taste from being impacted.  
⑩Evenly pushing and packing and bagging: Sampling should be timely conducted 
after holding out scented flowers, and evenly pushing and packing should be started 
timely after they are proved qualified. 
All of the above production processes are conducted within the prescribed geographic 
area. 
7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Relationship of Hengxian Mo Li Hua Cha quality characteristics and geographical 
origin:   
（1）Climate advantages: Heng County is located in the south of the Tropic of Cancer,

with typical subtropical monsoon climate, long summer and short winter, long 
frost-free season, adequate sunshine and rainfall, higher average temperatures, and 
small temperature difference between day and night, quite suitable for the growth of 
jasmine. Hengxian Mo Li Hua with the characteristics of early flowering ph ase and 
rich aroma, is sold in market in April, just the output period of spring tea. Its 
flowering phase lasts till the end of October.   
（2）Land advantages: Thanks to the high forest coverage rate of 43.9 ％in Heng 
County, Mo Li Hua soil in growing areas has high content of organics, with a soil pH 
of 6 to 6.5 and high fertility, very conducive to the growth of Mo Li Hua. As a result, 
Hengxian Mo Li Hua has large and plump buds, white and bright color and rich 
aroma, as well as high quality and yield.  
（3）Technical advantages: In the development of Hengxian Mo Li Hua Cha, through 
education, guidance and training, it has cultivated a batch of peasants mastering 
medium and high-yield Mo Li Hua planting skills and a group of workers proficient in 
Mo Li Hua Cha processing, laying a solid foundation for Jasmine production and 
scale development of tea processing and standardized production.  
（4）Industrial advantages: At present, the industrialization of Hengxian Mo Li Hua 
Cha production has reached a high level, and the industrial chain has been completed. 
Currently, Heng County has eight large Mo Li Hua trading market and the country's 
largest professional jasmine tea market -  Southwest Tea World, equipped with Mo Li 
Hua Cha, tea (tea base) and finished tea trading markets, involving more than 100,000 
mu Mo Li Hua land and over 150 flower (tea) processing enterprises.  
（5）Image advantages: Hengxian Mo Li Hua Cha is known for rich aroma, fresh and 
mellow taste, and Heng County is renowned as the Kingdom of Mo Li Hua of China, 
and has consolidated its image by holding the grand biennial National Mo Li Hua Cha 
Trade Fair and the annual China International Mo Li Hua Cultural Festival. 
（6）Unique processing technology: The production of Hengxian jasmine tea is made 
with traditional Chinese scenting techniques, usually storage scenting or pile scenting.

According to the different materials of tea embryos, the scenting times are different 
too. The more scent times, the stronger the aroma. The whole process includes ten 
working procedure which ensured the aroma of jasmine blend into the tea. They are 
Tea Embryo treatment, Fresh flower maintenance, Camellia mixing, Piling and 
scenting, Ventilation and scenting adding, Holding out of scented flowe r, Drying, 
Re-piling and re -scenting, Screening, Evenly pushing and packing . The scenting 
process of Hengxian jasmine tea is mainly the process of Hengxian jasmine flowers 
exhale aroma and tea embryos absorb the fragrance and it is an important link which 
can fully seize the exhaled aroma of Hengxian jasmine flowers and the absorbed 
fragrance of tea embryo. The exhaled aroma of Hengxian jasmine flowers is a 
biochemical change. Influential with enzyme, temperature, moisture and Oxygen etc, 
the mature jasmine  flowers decompose aromatic substances, and continuously 
exhaling aroma in the blossoming process. The absorbed fragrance of tea embryo is a 
physical process. When the tea embryo absorbs fragrance， it also absorbs a lot of 
water, therefore, complex chemical changes take place in hot and humid conditions. 
After processed by scenting, the color of the tea gradually changes from light green to 
bright yellow and the taste turns from acerbity to mellow, which forms the 
characteristics of Hengxian jasmine tea: rich of aroma, fresh and mellow taste.
