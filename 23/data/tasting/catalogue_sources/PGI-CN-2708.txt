5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Duobei Cha (Duobei Tea) is a famous tea brand with long history in southwest China. 
It is a kind of green tea made of fresh tea leaves of C. Sinensis cv. Anshun- zhuyecha 
and other tea  varieties grown in Puding County through traditional manufacturing 
procedure. It has some distinctive features. The dry tea product looks green bloom; 
the tea liquid is bright with heavy scent and tastes mellow and thick with sweet 
aftertaste.  
（1）Sensory Characteristics

6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
（1）Delimitation of the Geographical Area 
The growing area of Duobei Tea includes the following 5 administrative towns: 
Chengguan, Huachu, Maguan, Baiyan, Machang, and 6 township: Longchang, 
Maodong, Bulang, Pingshang, Jichangpo and Houchang in Puding County, Guizhou 
Province.  
（2）Manufacturing Procedure 
    Flat shape: fresh leaf picking → fresh leaf airing → fixation → carding → drying 
completely 
Curl shape: fresh leaf picking → fresh leaf airing → fixation → rolling → drying 
completely 
    Every step of the manufacturing procedure takes place in the defin ed 
geographical area in 6.1. 
7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Duobei Cha is one of the most famous teas in Guizhou province. It has a long history 
and enjoys high reputation in tea market in China. Duobei cha contains various

substances such as tea polytphenols, amino acids and catechins. The dry tea product 
looks green bloom. The liquid is bright with heavy scent and tastes mellow and thick 
with sweet aftertaste. Also the product leaves no tea stains. The special properties of  
Duobei cha are closely linked with the climate, soil, and human elements of the 
geographical area of Puding county delimited above.  
（1）Geographical Conditions and Climate  
Puding County lies at the middle of Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau. This area subjects to the 
influence of north subtropical monsoon humid climate. Therefore, it has four distinct 
seasons without extreme hot weather in summer nor severe cold in winter. The annual 
average temperature varies between 14 to 16℃ and 289 days in the year are frost-free. 
The average annual rainfall in this area reaches up to 1,396 mm, and it usually rains at 
night. The relative humidity reaches over 80%. This area is special in that it is cloudy 
and has short sunlight hours compared to other areas in China. The above f actors of 
elevation, sunshine hours, rainfall and humidity are favorable for quality tea growing, 
which determine the specificity of Duobei cha.  
（2）Soil Condition 
Duobei Cha plants are generally planted within mountainous area about the elevation 
of 1,200 – 1,600 meters. The major soil type there is yellow soil with thickness over 
50 centimeters. The soil pH value ranges from 4.5 to 6.0 and soil organic matter 
content is over 1.0%. These numbers indicate the best soil for growing tea. 
（3）Product Reputation and History  
Duobei Cha has a long history enjoys a high reputation. There are still many ancient 
but healthy tea plants in Puding county, two of which were estimated to have 
been around for more than a millennium, 20 were traced back to Yuan Dynasty (127 2 
-1368), 60 were dated back to Ming Dynasty(1368- 1644), and 240 Qing Dynasty 
(1644-1911). Duobei Cha became famous in Ming Dynasty as tribute tea till Qing 
Dynasty according to the record of Anshun Chronicle . In the year 1958, Duobei Cha  
was sent to natio nal agricultural product expo and was highly acclaimed. In 1960,
