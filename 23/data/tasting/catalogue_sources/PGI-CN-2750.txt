TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF  
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 桂平西山茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Guiping Xi Shan Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Guiping Xishan Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Guiping City  Agriculture Bureau Technology Promotion Center  (Address: No.23 
North Zhongshan Road , Xishan Town, Guiping City, Guigang City  , Guangxi) 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
The Agriculture Department Announcement No.1459, September 13, 2010 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Guiping Xishan Tea contains many nutrients including tea polyphenol, caffeinum, 
vitamin, amino acid, tea polysaccharide, and saponin, etc. It is characterized as a 
tender and fine tea with clear leaf tip and glossy indigo color. T he tea is green, clear 
and translucent. It has unique flavor and natural mellow, being sweetish, refreshing 
and fragrant after drinking. 
6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The protected scope for geographical  indication of Guiping Xishan Tea lies in east 
longitude 109°41′55″ to 110°22′48″, north latitude 22°52′02″ to 23°48′04″, and 
covers 26 towns and villages as well as one state -owned forestry station including 
Xishan Town, Mengxu Town, Shilong Town, Houlu Township, Baisha Town, Nanmu

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Guiping City covers an area of 4,074 square kilometers, in which there are 193,600 
hectares mountain areas which are suitable for growing tea. The PH value of arable 
soils is between 4.5 and 6.5, and the content of organic materials is between 2% and 
3%. Besides, the soil texture is loose with good permeability and ric h organic 
materials, which provide ideal conditions for the growth of teas. Guiping City  is 
located in the Tropic of Cancer and belongs to subtropics monsoon climate region 
with sufficient light, temperature and rainfall.  The average annual temperature is 
21.4°C in Guiping City whose climate is most favorable for the growth of 
high-quality tea for its mild climate, and for the tea picking season it has favorable 
relative humidity air above 80%, short sunshine and more diffuse light. The special 
climate and environment have contributed to the high quality of Guiping Xishan Tea. 
As one of the traditional green teas of Guangxi Province, Guiping Xishan Tea was 
named because it originated from the regions along the Guiping Xishan Mountain in 
Guangxi. Guiping Xishan Tea has a history of more than 1700 years; it first appeared 
in the Han and Jin dynasties, got flourished in the Tang Dynasty, and became famous 
in Fujian, Guangdong and Guangxi during the Ming Dynasty. Guiping Xishan Tea 
carries both rich cultural deposits and many legends. By virtue of its special flavor, 
men of literature of all dynasties have once wrote articles or poems to praise the tea.
