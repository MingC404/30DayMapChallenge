TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF  
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 福州茉莉花茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Fuzhou Mo Li Hua Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Fuzhou Jasmine Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Horticultural Society in Fuzhou City 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Registered as certification mark on January 7th, 2008 (registration number: 4939090). 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Fuzhou Jasmine Tea  is made of Fuzhou j asmine bud and green tea. Leaf shape tightly  
compact and showy ; green color and luster , white hairs throughout; strong, fresh, lovely, pure  
and long lasting fragrance; mellow and refreshing flavor, apparent sweet aftertaste; infusion 
color light green or yellow green and bright; infused leaves stout and strong, soft and bright, 
symmetrical. 
The specific characteristics of Fuzhou Jasmine T ea as defined are due to the natural factors 
in the delimited geographical area.

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Jasmine is a kind of tropical and subtropical long- day partial light demanding flower, likes 
sunshine, warm and wet area, hates shady, cold and waterlogging place. It is suitable for the 
environment with adequate illumination, an average temperature between 20℃ to 40℃, soil 
water content between 60% to 80%. Fuzhou is a warm and humid subtropical monsoon climate. 
Frost free period is up to 326 days. The climate is mild with annual average t emperature 
between 16 to 20℃ . The average temperature for the coldest January is 10.5 ℃, for the hotest 
July is 28.6℃. It is full of rains with the average humidity of 77% and annual average rainfall of 
1137.5mm. The unique chacter of Jasmine Tea depends on such above specific environment. 
For example, during the growing season of jasmine, the 75% to 80% air humidity is good to 
improving the productivity and quality of flowers. The relative humidity in Fuzhou is about 77%, 
which is suitable for the growth of  jasmine. Jasmine needs to grow at an environment not lower 
than 3 ℃ in winter. Although jasmine also grows in Jiansu and Zhejiang, but the winter 
temperature there is below 3 ℃, which increases the cost of jasmine flower. For its growth, 
Fuzhou provides the most suitable environment in light, heat, water and other aspects. 
In addition, the best jasmine tea needs the single petal jasmine flower produced in Fuzhou. 
This flower is pure in aroma, and the produced tea contains an unique ice sugar flavor. Fuzhou 
Jasmine Tea is made by a special scented process. Scented, also called smoked, is the use of tea 
to absorb jasmine’s aroma, and the conbination of tea flavor with flower fragrance. To produce a 
pure Fuzhou Jasmine Tea, if it is the nine scented tea, it need s 81 processes to smoked the 
flowers, which creates the fresh, tender character for the fragrance is lasting, the flavor is
