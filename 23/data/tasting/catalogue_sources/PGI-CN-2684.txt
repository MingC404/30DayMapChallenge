1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 正山小种 
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Zhengshan Xiao Zhong  
 
Translation for information purposes: Lapsang Souchong

Tea

Tea Research Institute of Wuyishan City 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Registered as certification mark on January 14, 2010 (registration number: 7430842). 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Lapsang Souchong is thick and straight, with strong leaf, strong bones and no core. It  
has a lasting and pleasant smell of burning pine wood and dried longan. It is grouped 
in smoked souchong and non-smoked souchong by production process whether using 
pine needles or pine firewood to smoke. Using pine needles or firewood to smoke is 
called “Smoked Lapsang Souchong”. The other is called “Non -smoked Lapsang 
Souchong”. Distinctive d ifferences exist for the two groups of teas for their different 
production processes. 
First, from the appearance, Non-smoked Lapsang Souchong is in dark brown, and the 
smoked one for the reason of smoking, is more black and moist. 
Second, from the soup, the colour of Non- smoked Lapsang Souchong is bright red, 
and the smoked one is reddish brown. 
Last, from the taste, two groups of Lapsang Souchong have obvious sweetness, but 
the smoked one has a significant flavour of longan and tastes more sweet and with a 
special unique pine soot aroma.

The specific characteristics of Lapsang Souchong  as defined are due to the natural

6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The 11 towns (villages), streets and farms in the administrative division of Wuyishan 
include Xingcun Town, Wuyi Street, Xingtian Town, Wufu Town, Chong’an Town, 
Yangzhuang Village, Langu Village, Shangmei Village, Xinfeng Street and Zonghe 
Farm. 
7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The producing area of Lapsang  Souchong, located at the core of the natural 
conservation area of Mount Wuyi, is among peaks that rise higher than one another. 
The annual average temperature is - 10 to -8℃, the diurnal temperature variation is 6 
to 10 degrees , and the frost season lasts for 90 to 100 days a year. The fertile soil, 
short hours of daylight, sufficient rainfall and the suitable varieties for the local 
condition cultivated by the residents  in the past several hundred years  provide 
Lapsang Souchong with quality leaves to serve as ingredients.  
According to Chinese Tea Classics, “Lapsang” in Lapsang Souchong refers to the 
production from real mountainous area. Tea made from Wuyi mountain are called 
Lapsang Souchong, similar one from adjacent or other areas are called Waishan (outer 
hills) Souchong. The strong leaf is a m ajor feature of Lapsang Souchong. For the 
production area is in high altitude, the growth of tea tree is slower than that in outer 
hills. Generally, tea leaves are picked after May when they are thick and big. The 
production area has fertile soil. Local fa rmers have a habit to remove soil from other 
places to improve and enhance local soil. For that reason, tea trees flourish with thick 
and soft leaves. 
Fresh leaves for Lapsang Souchong wither by warming. There are lots of pine in local 
area, and pine firewood is used for daily fuel, therefore withering fuel is also the pine 
firewood. The withering is made in Wuyi mountain’s unique building, which has two 
layers without floor but divided by crossbar files. The gap between two nearby files is 
about 3- 4cm. Bam boo seats with small square holes are set up on the files for 
spreading the withered leaves. Baking racks are set up for smoking under 0.3m of the

files. During withering process, wet pine firewood is established on group in a T 
shape or a line shape, after ignition, it is burned slowly and warm and soften the fresh 
tea leaves by hot airs produced by wet blank set up on the lower baking rack. The 
fresh leaves are s pread evenly on wittering seats  which is on the files and warmed by 
gaps of square holes. Leaves are spread in 3 -7cm thickness. Doors and windows are 
closed to avoid heat loss. Room temperature is controlled at about 30 degrees. Leaves 
are mixed every about 20 minutes with a quick action to void injury of leaves. The 
warming fire is required to be evenly also. For sunny days, sunshine withering is also 
carried out on a green seat hanging on files of 2.5m high. Mixing the leaves two to 
three times to disperse water evenly. The withering process is finished when the 
leaves loose original brightness, l eaves become soft and touched like the cotton, stem 
unbreakable, veins transparent, keeping moderate fragrance. Withered leaves are 
required to be slightly cooled in indoor ground before rolling.
