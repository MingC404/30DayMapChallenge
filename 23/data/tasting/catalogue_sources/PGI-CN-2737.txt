GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 泰顺三杯香茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Taishun San Bei Xiang Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Taishun Three Cups of Incense Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Tea Association of Taishun County 
Address: No.160, Taifen Road, Taishun County, Wenzhou City, Zhejiang Province. 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Announcement No. 1517 of the Ministry of Agriculture of the People's Republic of 
China 
Date of Issuance: December 24, 2010 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
The tea leaves are thin and tight in appearance and light green  in color, the tea water 
is bright , green and clear. The fragrance is strong and long -lasting, the taste is  
palatable and mellow, and the tea dregs tender and wel l-distributed. According to the 
test result, Taishun Sanbeixiang (Three Cups of Incense) has an amino acid content of 
over 2.5% and a tea polyphenol content of 9%~10%.  
6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Taishun Sanbeixiang is produced in Taishun County of Wenzhou City. It is located 
119°37′to 120°15 ′east longitude and 27°17 ′to 27°50 ′north latitude (the center at 
27°33′N and 119°42′E).

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Taishun County is located in the southeast of the Donggong Mountain and features 
low-middle mountainous landforms with overlapping and winding hills within the 
area, and is known as the “ ridge of south Zhejiang” . The county is located in the 
subtropical marine monsoon climate zone with distinct four seasons, mild climate, 
and abundant rainfall; rainfall and high temperature come at the same time in spring  
and summer, and sunshine and heat complement each other in autumn and winter; 
high mountains are enveloped in cloud and mist with distinct elevations, resulting in 
complex landforms and terrains and varied microclimate. According to the integrated 
data from 1981 to 2010, the annual temperature here is 16.2℃, the extreme minimum 
temperature is - 10.5℃, the extreme maximum temperature is 39.2 ℃, the frost -free 
period is 331.5 days, the annual sunshine duration i s 1627.7 hours, the effective 
accumulated temperature is 4999.1℃, the annual average precipitatio n is 2047.5 mm, 
and the annual average relative humidity is 82.1%. The soil in Taishun County can be 
classified into many types which are distributed across different landforms. Generally, 
there are five soil groups, nine soil sub- groups, 25 soil genus, and 55 soil species; the 
soils in tea gardens mostly belong to red loam and yellow loam with high content of 
organic matter. The forest coverage is 75.6% and the  greening rate is 77.24%. For 
many years, the comprehensive environment quality of Taishun County has been 
ranking first in Zhejiang Province, which provides favorable conditions  for the 
synthesis of the tea trees ’ active ingredients. As a result, Sanbeixi ang tea  leaves are 
slender and tight with keen- edged apex; in even and moderate size, the leaves are 
freshly green, giving off the special but enduring smell of tender shoots. The tea water 
is bright, green and clear, and the taste is both fresh and mellow . Tea experts once 
appraised it as “ clear and pleasant to the eye with long -lasting fragrance even after 
being rinsed with three cups of water ”. Sanbeixiang also has wide -spread popularity 
with customers. 
8. SPECIFIC RULES CONCERNING LABELLING AND USING (IF ANY) 
The indication should be accorded with the requirements issued by Center for Quality 
and Safety of Agri -products of Ministry of Agriculture. Uniformed geographic 
indication of agricultural products shall be used on either products or packaging (the 
name, public logos, and way of marking).
