TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF  
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 千岛银珍  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Qiandao Yin Zhen  
 
Translation for information purposes: Qiandao Rare Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Qiandao Yinzhen Tea Cooperatives of Jiande City 
Address: Binjiang Village, Meicheng Town, Jiande City, Zhejiang Province  
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Announcement No. 1517 of the Ministry of Agriculture of the People's Republic of 
China Date of Issuance: December 24, 2010 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Qiandao Rare Tea is tight  and straight as needle and is tender  green in color. The 
color of the tea water is clear and bright. The aroma of the tea is clean and fresh, and 
the taste is sweet and mellow. The tea leaves are tender and even.  After being tested, 
the amino acid content of Qiandao Rare Tea is greater than 2.0%, and the tea 
polyphenols content is 9 % -10 %.  
6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Qiandao Rare Tea is produced from the Qiandao Lake, the Xin'an River, the Lanjiang 
River and the Fuchun River  in Jiande City. It is located  29 ° 12'20 " to 29 ° 46'27" 
north latitude and 118 ° 53'46 " to 119 ° 45'51"  east longitude. The raw materials of

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The specific characteristics of Qiandao Yinzhen as defined under point 5 are due to 
the natural factors and the skills and knowledge of the local producers in the delimited 
geographical area.                             
Jiande has a subtropical monsoon climate. It has four distinct seasons which is warm 
and humid, with a lot of rainfall. Because of high elevation gaps and many mountains, 
it has many clouds and large temperature difference, A nnual average temperature is 
16 ℃, a nnual average rainfall is 1650mm and annual average relative humidity is 
78%. The frost-free period throughout a year lasts 252 days with 1940h of sunshine 
duration, which is good for the tea tree to accumulate nutrients . The Mountain soil of 
Jiande develops from parent material composed of sandstone, limestone weathering 
mostly deposit and alluvial soil. There are mainly yellow soil, red -yellow soil and 
sandy soil, most of which is acidic or slightly acidic. The soil is fe rtile and its organic 
matter content is 2% to 4%. The unique natural environment of Jiande is very suitable 
for the growth of Qiandao Rare Tea trees. The soil quality is good and the nutrient 
proportion is harmonious, which can provide the rich mineral nut rient for the tea tree 
growth. The tea garden is shrouded in mist all year round, surrounded by dense 
evergreen vegetation with more diffuse light. The temperature of the tea garden has 
great differences between day and night  .All these are beneficial to n itrogen 
metabolism and secondary metabolism of tea trees. Tea is rich in amino acids and 
aromatic substances. Presented in terms of quality, the taste of Qiandao Rare Tea is 
fresh and fragrant.
