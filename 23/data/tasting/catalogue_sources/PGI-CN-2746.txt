TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF  
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 麻城福白菊  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Macheng Fu Bai Ju  
 
Translation for information purposes: Macheng Chrysanthemum Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Other products of Annex I to the Treaty (spices etc.) - Tea  
3. APPLICANT 
Blessing Chrysanthemum Industry Association of Macheng (Address: No. 1, Juxiang 
Road, Futianhe Town, Macheng City, Hubei Province)  
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Announcement No. 1054 of the Ministry of Agriculture 
Date of Issuance: July 1, 2008 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
"Macheng chrysanthemum" features the following quality characteristics: it has big 
hypertrophy flowers, the petal is as white as jade, the stamen is in deep yellow color, 
the flower soup is clear and in golden yellow with green color, it presents faint scent, 
sweet and mellow taste, etc. A single flower of it is 2 -5cm big, the petals are about 
22-25mm long and 5-7mm wide, the contents of total flavonoids and chlorogenic acid 
in the flowers are high, and it belongs to Chinese traditional medicine that serves both 
as food and medicine. "Macheng chrysanthemum" contains total flavonoids of 3.0- 8.2% 
and chlorogenic acid of 0.18-0.35%.  
6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The production area of "Macheng chrysanthemum" is located in the mountainous area

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The production area of "Macheng chrysanthemum" lies in the tributary of the Yangtze 
River – the source location of Jushuihe River, the water quality is good and free of 
any industrial pollution. Its soil features the characteristics of yellow -brown soil with 
typical transition from the north to the south –  the soil is deep and presents good 
performance of water conservation. The soil organic matter content is 1.5%, 
alkali-hydrolyzable nitrogen is 82ppm, organic phosphorus is 11.9ppm, available 
potassium is 165ppm, and PH value ranges between 6.0- 7.5. It is located at the massif 
and river valley areas which are 200m to 800m above sea level. The annual average 
temperature is 13 -16 degrees Celsius, the annual average sunshine hours range 
between 1634 -2249 hours, the annual average precipitation ranges between 
1112.2-1688.7 mm, and the annual frost -free period reaches 237 days. Futianhe Town 
is located at the boundary ridge between the Yangze River and the Huai River; the 
climate here is characterized by four distinct seasons, moderate rainfall, and 
synchronization between water, light and heat. Futianhe Town has a long history of 
planting chrysanthemum and has been appraised as “ the home to health boosting 
chrysanthemum”, and chrysanthemum industry has become a characteristic and pillar 
industry for Macheng City a nd even Dabieshan area. The unique geological and 
environmental conditions as well as the favorable climate in Futianhe Town are very 
suitable for the growth of chrysanthemum, which, in addition to the standardized 
planting and processing technologies, “ Macheng chrysanthemum Tea” has become a

8. SPECIFIC RULES CONCERNING LABELLING AND USING (IF ANY) 
As for the production and usage of registered or protected labels of geographical 
indication of “Macheng Chrysanthemum” on the products or packages, the producers 
or operators of “Macheng Chrysanthemum ” within this territory should file an 
application to the registration certificate holder, Chrysanthemum Industry Association 
of Macheng, t o produce and use the labels, or utilize the united labeling method by 
combing the product name with the agricultural product ’s geographical indication in 
accordance with relevant regulations.
