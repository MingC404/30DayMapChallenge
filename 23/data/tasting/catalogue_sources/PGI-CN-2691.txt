TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF  
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
Chinese name: 松溪绿茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Songxi Lü Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Songxi Green Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Tea Management Station in Songxi County 
 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Registered as certification mark on May 28th, 2009(registration number: 6534378). 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
In certain natural environment, superior geographical conditions, good air quality and 
high altitude mountain area, Songxi green tea is cultivated. The tea leaves are green 
and well -shaped with persistent aroma. The soup is bright yellow and green and it 
tastes strong. The tea leaves are well -developed after brewing, and can be brewed for 
several times. Songxi green tea can be categorized into 4 types based on the shape of 
leaves: bud shape, flat  shape, curved shape and strip shape. The characteristic of the 
tea is: 
1. Bud shape "Songxi green tea": maintain the natural shape of single bud, fat and 
straight the color is silver -white and green; the inner aroma is fresh and  fragrant, 
the soup is apricot green, the taste is fresh and sweet, and the bottom of the leave

2, flat "Songxi green tea": the shape is flat and straight, smooth and even, the color is silver grey

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
The specific characteristics of the Songxi green tea as defined under point 5 are due to the 
natural factors in the delimited geographical area. 
The best climate condition for tea trees’ growth is that the monthly average temperature is above 
18 <> C, annual effective accumulation temperature is above 4000 o C, annual average rainfall is 
1000-2000mm, and relative humidity is 80- 90%. Songxi is a subtropical monsoon climate with 
monthly average temperature 18» C, annual accumulated temperature of 6250o C, and effective 
temperature above 10o C is 5542 0 C, annual average rainfall of 1753mm, annual average 
humidity of about 80%. Songxi is located in the source of Minjiang River and south foot of Wuyi 
Mountain, mostly red loam soil with PH value between 4.5 and 5.5, and organic matter content
