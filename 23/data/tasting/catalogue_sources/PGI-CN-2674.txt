TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION OF 
GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION 
 
1. NAME OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL INDICATION   
Chinese name: 婺源绿茶  
 
Transcription in Latin characters: Wuyuan Lü Cha  
 
Translation for information purposes: Wuyuan Green Tea  
 
2. CATEGORY OF THE PRODUCT FOR WHICH THE NAME IS PROTECTED 
Tea 
3. APPLICANT 
Tea Association in Wuyuan County 
4. PROTECTION IN CHINA 
Registered as certification mark  on January 14th, 2007 ( registration number : 
4864293). 
5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
Wuyuan Green Tea has tight leaves and is jade green in color. It has enduring 
faint scent as well as fresh and moistening taste. The tea water color is clear and 
bright. The leaf bottom is vividly green and neat. The water content accounts for less 
than or equal to 7.0%, total ash takes up less than or equal to 7.0%, powder occupies 
less than or equal to 1.0% and the water extracts hold more than or equal to 36.0%. 
The spring t ea has a bud with two leaves , containing amino acid  5.5%, tea 
polyphenols 22.6%, caffeine 4.4%, catechuic acid14.6%。 
The specific characteristics of Wuyuan Green Tea as defined are due to the 
natural factors in the delimited geographical area. 
 6. CONCISE DEFINITION OF THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA

Wuyuan Green Tea is produced in 16 towns covering 171 administrative villages 
including Duanshen, Xitou, Jiangwan, Qiukou, Da Zhangshan, Tuochuan, Zheyuan, 
Qinghua, Sikou, Ziyang, Taibai, Zhongyun, Fuchun, Xucun, Zhen Zhushan, Zhentou. 
They are within the jurisdiction of Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province. 
The total planting area covers 10300 hectares. Located in the northeast of Jiangxi 
Province, Wuyuan County adjoins Kaihua County of Zhejiang Province on the  east, 
Dexing City and Leping City on the south, Fuliang County on the west and Xiuning 
County of Anhui Province on the north. Wuyuan County has a longitude range of 
117°22' to 118°11' E and a latitude range of 29°01' to 29°35' N. 
7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Within the border of Wuyuan County there are three landforms, namely middle 
mountain, low mountain and hills with low hills as the main form. The soil, of which 
90% is red-yellow soil, has a PH value ranging from 4.5- 6.5 and deep layers with rich 
nutrients. The soil can be permeated easily and has abundant underground water 
which is of good quality. Four seasons have distinct features in that winter is cold and 
summer is hot. Annual average rainfall is 1821mm, annual average temperature is  
16.7℃ and sunshine rate is 44%. Moreover, the frost -free period lasts for 252 days, 
which provides advantaged conditions for the growth of Wuyuan Green Tea. As the 
most beautiful village of China, hometown of Chinese tea and core producing area of 
the gol den triangle of Chinese green tea, Wuyuan is given a reputation as a place 
where green bush spreads all over the mountain and tea fragrance can be smelled from 
every household. Tea producing activities in Wuyuan started in the Han Dynasty and 
prospered in the Tang Dynasty. Tea culture goes back to ancient times and has deep 
cultural heritage. In 2007, Wuyuan Green Tea was honored as “ Chinese Well-known 
Agricultural Product ”. Characterized by its green and natural color, strong and 
fragrant taste and clear w ater, Wuyuan Green Tea is exported to overseas destinations 
including EU, the United States, Canada and Japan. Moreover, it has occupied 70% of 
EU market share of organic green tea, bearing a high reputation at home and abroad.
