5. Description of product    
（1） Sensory Characteristics 
Anji Bai Cha, made of a particular tea vari eties "Baiye No.1" fresh leaves , is a 
kind of green tea. It has the characteristics of tender bud with one -to-three white 
leaves. When it is brewed, the beverage is tender green and bright with long -lasting

fragrance, mellow and brisk taste, and the infused leaves is whole shoot with white 
leaves and jade green vein. 
According to the appearance, it is divided into dragon shape and phoenix shape. 
According to the sensory characteristics, it is divided into boutique grade, special 
grade, first grade and second grade.   
（2）Physical and chemical properties  
Tea Moisture/（%）≤6.5  
Dust tea/（%）≤1.2  
Total ash /（%）≤6.5 
Crude fibre/（%）≤10.5 
 Water extract/（%）≥32  
Free amino acide/(by Glutamic acid)（%）≥5.0 
（3）Raw material information and requirements:  
The fresh leaves collected from "Baiye No.1" in the mid  or end March to the 
mid-April, should be an intact bud with early leaf to three leaves, and the leaves 
should be white with jade green vein.  
6. Concise definition of the geographical area 
The delimitation of the geographical area: Administrative region of Anji county,

The specific production steps: 
（1）Phoenix shape ：Tedding fresh leaves→ Fixation→ Tidying tea strip→ 
Rolling tea→ First drying→ Re- drying→ Refinement 
（2）Dragon shape：Tedding fresh leaves→ Fixation → Leaf cooling→ Drying 
7. Link with the Geographical Area

The quality of Anji Bai Cha is the content of tea polyphenols was low and the 
content of amino acid was high, which has  closely related to the climate, soil, 
environment factors of Anji.   
（1）Relationship with the natural geography  
The Anji county  located at  30°23′~30°52′ N and 119°14′~119°53′ E, is a basin 
surrounded by mountains on three sides. Due to the geographical environment, the  
leaves of Anji Bai Cha  is white with  jade green vein , and the Anji Bai Cha  has 
low ratio of tea polyphenols to amino acids  . (The ratio of tea polyphenols to amino 
acids of tea, which is an important index to the green tea taste. Under the certain 
content of tea polyphenols, the lower the ratio of polyphenols to amino acids, the 
better the quality of tea.) 
（2）Relationship with the climate 
With the north subtropical monsoon climate, Anji has distinct four seasons. All 
the year round, average temperature is 15.5 ℃ , the coldest average temperature in 
January is - 1 ℃ ~ 3 ℃; About 1510 mm annual rainfall, relative humidity 80%, 
sunshine time 2000 h, frost -free period 226 d. When the environment temperature is 
19-23℃,the fresh tea leaves turn albescent, and then the content of soluble protein in 
albescent leaves increas ed and the content of tea polyphenols decreased. When the 
temperature over 25℃,  the fresh tea leaves turn green again, and the content of 
soluble protein decreased and the content of tea polyphenols increased. 
So, Anji Bai Cha processed with albescent leav es has low  ratio of tea 
polyphenols to amino acids and fine appearance. 
（3）Relationship with the soil

Anji County is rich in mountain resources, vegetation coverage was 73%, and the 
forest coverage rate reached 69%. The  Quaternary red yellow soil with high content 
of organic compounds, pH4.5 ~ 6.5, highly suitable for the tea tree growth. 
（3）Relationship with the Human factor.Anji Bai Cha can be traced back to 
about 1000 years ago in song dynasty. In August 1980, tea science and technology 
workers Liu Yi -min found a clump of white tea tree of more than one hundred years 
old in Anji Tianhuangpi ng village. After cutting breeding and demonstration 
promotion, white tea cultivation area are over 100 thousand mu in Anji now. Tea 
science experts Zhuang Wan -fang acclaimed Anji Bai Cha of ornamental, nutrition 
and economic value to a body, is a rare tre asures in tea ". Anji Bai Cha is an exotic 
flower in Chinese green tea, enjoys a good reputation both at home and abroad.
