5. DESCRIPTION OF PRODUCT 
    Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea)'s raw materials are planted and harvested 
within the Yingde administrative district, after the processing steps of 
withering,twisting(twisting-cutting), fermentation and dehydrat ion, the Yingde Hong 
Cha (Yingde Black Tea) has been produced. Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) 
has fat and black bloom colour appearance, forming a high long lasting sweet aroma, 
and liquor color is red and bright, with mellow and thick taste and sweet aftertaste. It 
can be classified into top class (grade), first class (grade) or second class (grade) by its 
sensory qualities.

（3）Information on Raw Material 
Raw material must come from the definition habitat scope: The four growing regions, 
Yingdong, Yingzhong, Yingxibei, Yingxinan of Guangdong Yingde City. 
 Species and Varieties: Yunnan big-leaf, Phoenix Narcissus, Yinghong No.6, Yinghong 
No.1, Wulinghong, Xiuhong, and other species and varieties of Daye Black Tea which 
is approved by either nation or the province that are suitable for producing Yingde 
Black Tea. 
Plucking fresh leaves:  
Fresh leaves for whole leaf black tea: picking single bud, or one  leaf and one bud, or 
two leaves and one bud, or three leaves and one bud, or symmetry  leaves with the 
equally tenderness; 
Fresh leaves for broken black tea: picking two leaves and one bud, or three leaves and 
one bud, or symmetry leaves with the equally tenderness.

Main Tea Process Technology: 
（1） Equipment: withering trough, tea rolling machine , twisting and cutting machine 
(model 7051), tea  block-separating and sifting machine, 
fermentation equipment, drying machine,  tea plane-surface round sifter, tea 
winnowing machine and tea stalk-sorting machine 
（2） Process of Black Tea 
①Processing procedure: fresh tea leaves  → withering → twisting → fermentation → 
dehydration 
②Processes： 
Withering: from 12 to 24 hours, the Water Content is 56% to 58% 
Twisting:  about 60 to 90 minutes totally, and divided into 3 periods, each period is 
30 minutes, tea should be processed by the tea block- separating and sifting machine 
for 1 to 2 times during this process. 
Fermentation：for 3 to 5 hours  
Dehydration：from 110 ℃ to 120℃ for the first firing ，90℃ to 95℃ for the full 
firing；the water content of the semi-finished tea should be less or equal to 6.5% after 
dehydration 
（3）Process of  Broken Black Tea： 
① Processing Procedure ： fresh leaves → withering → twisting-cutting → 
fermentation → dehydration。 
②Processing technology index：  
Withering: about 8 to 18hours, the Water Content is 60% to 62% 
Twisting-cutting for the first time: sifted and collect the No.2-1 tea base, the tea leaves 
in the first round should be more than 50%, the second round 45% -50%, the third 
round less than 45%, and the last is less than 10% 
Re-twisting-cutting: sifted and collect the No.3 -1 tea bas e, the tea leaves in the first 
round should be more than 25%, the second round 25% -30%, the third round more

7. LINK WITH THE GEOGRAPHICAL AREA 
Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea), Dianhong and Qihong are jointly known as the 
top three black teas in China. Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) is broken black 
tea that made of tea leaves of Yunnan big leaves specie, and is the important pillar of 
the second set of broken black tea, and to be called as the “ Rising star of Chinese 
Black Tea”.  The appearance of Yingde Ho ng Cha (Yingde Black Tea) is shown with 
black bloom colour, red and bright liquor，and the taste is heavy and sweet. It has the 
sweet aftertaste similar to Dianhong, and the aroma is heavy and pure, with the fresh 
bright infused leaf, which differs from Qihong in style. The quality and characteristic 
of Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) is closely linked with various factors such as 
the climate, soil, environment, cultural of Yingde Area： 
（1）Link with Geographical Feature: 
Yingde City is located in the nort hwest of Guangdong province,  at the 
middle-stream of the North River, with its geographic area located between 112° 45′ ~ 
113° 55′ east longitude and 23º50´~ 24º33´north latitude. Yingde City is located in the 
south rim of the Wuling mountains, and is the  mountain hills region of Nanling 
Mountains, with its Northside next to Ruyuan Yao Autonomous County and Quyang 
County, bounded by Wengyuan County and Xinfeng County in the east, Fogang and 
Qingxin in the south and southwest, and it borders with Yangshan i n the  northwest. 
The city topography is about 118 kilometers  long from east to west, and 
112 kilometers wide in south- north direction, which covers an total area of 5671 
square kilometres, and this is the county -level city with the largest land area withi n 
Guangdong Province. The city topography can be divided into four types of area:

mountains of middle height area (1000 M above  sea-level), low mountains area 
(500-1000M sea -level), hill country area (around 500M sea -level) and plateaus and

geographical environment, Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) has fine quality 
such as black bloom colour, red and bright liquor ，heavy and sweet taste, heavy and 
pure aroma etc.  
（2）Link with Climate Feature 
The terrains of Yingde city is higher in the north and lower in the south, and located in 
the intermediate area from south subtropical to tropical, which shows bioclimatic 
transitional characteristics. With south -subtropical monsoon cl imate, Yingde city is 
warm and rainy, neither extremely cold winter nor hot summer, and the annual 
average temperature is 20.7°C. July is the hottest month of the year with average 
temperature being 28.8°C, and January is the coldest month with average tem perature 
being 10.7°C. The mean value of annual rainfall is 1876.8mm, average rainfall days 
are 162, the average relative humidity is 79%, and about 316.7 days in the year are 
frost-free. Such unique climate feature develops the Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Bla ck 
Tea)’s characteristic: grainy tightly heavy body, bloom colour, tender and even, 
golden tip was revealed, aroma fresh pure and heavy, with dominant notes of flowery 
flavor, heavy and thick and sweet taste, red and bright liquor colour with a clear 
golden ring, and the infused leaf soft and bright.   
（3）Link with soil conditions 
The soil conditions are quite advantageous for growing tea bush. The gentle slopes 
and plateaus between hills and foothills are all red and yellow soil, the soil is naturally 
fertile, the organic matter and nitrogen content of the soil is above average level, and 
there is mostly acidic soil with PH 4-5.5 which makes it perfect to grow tea bush. 
（4）Link with cultural characteristics 
Yingde is an old tea region with a long tea produc ing history. According to the 
historical records that the history of tea producing can be traced back to as early as the

Tang Dynasty about 1200 years ago. According to the China ’s first tea monographs 
<The Classic of Tea – Eight: the producing area>writte n by YuLu that “ Tea from 
south of the Five Ridges are produced in Fuzhou, Quanzhou, Zhaozhou and 
Xiangzhou, …, sometimes I can obtain tea from these areas, and they taste very good”. 
Yingde back then is the major producer in Shaozhou. Also, the research of  Qikun 
Chen and Xuelan Zhuang, that Yingde start producing tea in Tang Dynasty, and is one 
of the three producing area in Shaozhou. Thur Yingde has a long-tea producing history. 
As early as the Ming Dynasty, tea was offered to the palace as a tribute. And in Qing 
Dynasty, Yingde tea producing had already become popular among the people, and 
became a flourished international trading product during early 19th century. In 1959, 
Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) was successfully trial -produced. With the fine 
quality such as black bloom colour, red and bright liquor ，heavy and sweet taste, 
heavy and pure aroma etc, Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) is famous around 
the world and rated highly. Enjoying Yingde Hong Cha (Yingde Black Tea) becomes a 
trend for successful people and insiders.
