# Day 18 preview: sources and use notices

This is a local composition study created on 2026-11-19. The organisations named below have not endorsed it.

The real elevation source is Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public from the AWS public COG distribution. It is a nominal 30 metre digital **surface** model, not Taiwan MOI's 20 metre bare-earth DTM. Horizontal reprojection, cropping, hillshade, contours, terrain interpretation and colour styling were performed for this preview. The vertical datum remains EGM2008.
