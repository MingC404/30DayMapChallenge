# Historical harbor sources

Original NYPL downloads are preserved without recompression or retouching. Day06 uses `nypl_434593_original.jpg` (4598 × 4344 px), **New York Harbor and entrance: from the U.S. Coast Surveys**, E. & G.W. Blunt, New York. [Catalog](https://old-digitalcollections.nypl.org/items/510d47da-ef96-a3d9-e040-e00a18064a99) · [Original JPEG](https://iiif-prod.nypl.org/index.php?id=434593&t=g). The catalog date is **1844 (questionable)**, not a verified survey year.

The unused `nypl_434585_original.jpg` and its catalog HTML remain original candidate-source records, not duplicate final exports. `candidate_sources.json` preserves the download URLs and original hashes for both candidates. NYPL considers these items public domain in the US, without determining rights in other countries.

`control_reference/` holds small OSM API extracts and acquisition records used only to identify masonry reference centers. `modern_land/` holds the complete Upper New York Bay water relation and its source record, added to existing Hudson River and Morris Canal Basin caches. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Download dates and OSM object revisions are not survey dates.

Third-party multilingual values and institutional page navigation are deliberately unchanged. The final map and English source/method summary are in [Day06](../../06/README.md).
