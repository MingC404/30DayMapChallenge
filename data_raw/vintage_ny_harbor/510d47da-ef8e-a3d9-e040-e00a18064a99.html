Notes
Content: "Topography engraved by S. Siebert & A. Rolle ; views engraved by O.A. Lawson ; engraving supervised & views of the coast drawn by J. Farley ; hydrography engraved by F. Dankworth ; lettering by F. Dankworth & J. Knight."
Content: In upper right margin: Electrotype copy no. 7, by G. Mathiot U.S.C.S.
Content: Includes text, table of currents, and 4 coastal panoramas.
Content: National Endowment for the Humanities Grant for Access to Early Maps of the Middle Atlantic Seaboard.
Content: Prime Meridians: New York and Greenwich.
Citation/reference: Cohen, P.E. Manhattan in maps, 1527-1995, p. 122
Content: Relief shown by hachures; depths shown by soundings.
Statement of responsibility: founded upon a trigonometrical survey under the direction of F.R. Hassler, superintendent of the Survey of the Coast of the United States ; triangulation by James Ferguson and Edmund Blunt, assistants ; the hydrography under the direction of Thomas R. Gedney, lieutenant U.S. Navy ; the topography by C. Renard, T.A. Jenkins & B.F. Sands assists. ; A.D. Bache superintendent ; verified by Lieut. A.A. Humphreys, topl. engrs. assistant, U.S. Coast Survey.
