Methodology & sources

Continuous data mode keeps the original light timing. No 12-second phase reset or seam fade is applied to navigation-light records.

In the 12-second artistic loop, only lights with periods that do not divide 12 are gently faded to location dots over the first/last 1.2 seconds. Their underlying SIGSEQ is not rescaled; their display phase restarts at the seam. This export is not a fully faithful temporal reproduction.

141 original sequences, one Iso equal-phase definition, eight steady lights and three dim untimed markers. Colors come from audited USCG / NOAA light attributes. Actual inter-light phase is unknown; the common zero is illustrative. Five lighthouse effects are separate artistic layers; they are not extra registered lights.

Romer and Coney both rotate in 12 seconds here, with different artistic starting angles. Their historical 20/30-second periods are research context only and are not played in this edition. West Bank keeps documented fixed red/white sectors and 3 s light / 3 s dark; Robbins Reef keeps 0.6 s light / 5.4 s dark; Sandy Hook stays steady.

153 / 142 / 5 refer to registered light-list entries, reproducible rhythms and entries with documented sectors. They are not physical fixture counts or counts of rotating beams. Conditional lights are not asserted to be operating now.

Land / water masks adjust artistic contrast only, not terrain occlusion or real visibility. Beam length is never taken from nominal range.
