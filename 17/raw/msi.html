The U.S. Coast Guard Navigation Center announces Local Notice to Mariners Special Notices, which contains important updates to navigational safety and maritime information published in the most recent LNM. Mariners should review these Special Notices in conjunction with the current LNM to ensure compliance with all applicable navigation requirements and regulations.
