311: Jan–Dec 2025 · POIs: research snapshot ·

Study area: six adjoining SoHo blocks between Prince and Broome, from Mercer to Lafayette, with Spring, Broadway and Crosby inside. 18 OSM potential sources and 12 historical odor/fumes complaints at 10 distinct coordinates. Seven indoor complaints are excluded from outdoor propagation. Sanitation and public-urination behavior are not mapped as confirmed odors; no urine-odor observations are asserted. Nearby businesses are not linked to complaints.

Explore Space has one cursor-driven, root-connected geometry. Colors are cached from the nearest building-constrained path to each evidence class, independently normalized to unit peak; duplicate complaint coordinates are collapsed. A compact kernel cuts off at an artistic path budget of 150, with limited evidence below 0.12 shown neutral. These are not odor concentration units. Geometry and evidence are computed separately.

Propagation uses a conservative 2 m building-obstacle grid, weighted Dijkstra paths and Marching Squares. A 110 m geometry-only margin allows routes outside the visible boundary. All sources share one initial Spread budget. The budget and color have no measured concentration or smell-distance interpretation. Model-open space is not necessarily publicly accessible; walls and closed passages may be missing. Source positions may be address geocodes or POI centers. Computational relocation is disclosed on selection; relocations over 20 m are suppressed. Overlapping transparent layers remain separate illustrations, not a new odor class.
